## 8. Observations

- **Macro aggregates dominate.** 31 of 41 items are macro series (WDI, KNBS, IMF, ILOSTAT). No figure is computed from microdata in the report: Fig 29 reuses published poverty rates, and MSME statistics (pp. 34–35) are text only.
- **Many charts are reused.** About 9 items come from other documents (Figs 5, 17, 24, 25, 27, 29, 30, 38a, 38b), so the suite needs a transcribe/digitize fallback.
- **Vintages matter.** Figs 14, 22 and 29 use 2017 PPP (current WDI: 2021 PPP). ILO vintage is Nov 2023 in source lines but Nov 2022 in fn 24. KNBS exports stop at 2023, before the 2025 revisions. PWT ends in 2019.
- **The analysis window is old.** Many analytical charts stop in 2019 (Figs 4, 20, 28, 35); the text and Fig 16 run to mid-2026.
- **Titles and axes disagree in several places:**
  - Fig 6: title starts at 2006/07, the axis at 2005/06.
  - Fig 16: title period ≠ axis period, and the "Jan-24" labels repeat.
  - Fig 19: title says 2015/16–2023/24, the axis 2016/17–2024/25.
  - Fig 23: 2021–2023 in the title vs 2021–2024 in the legend.
  - Fig 28: 2007=100 in the title vs 2000=100 on the axis.
  - Fig 2: the 2020 stacks net to about +1.2 pp, but the label says g = 0.5%.
  - Fig 38: one legend colour does not match its bar segment.
  - Fig 37: the headline is about interest rates, which the chart does not show.
- **Text and charts disagree in places.** A nonexistent "figure 3.3" is cited (p.22). Real wage growth 2010–2019 is 2.1% in text vs ~4.6% on the chart. Poverty is 42.9% vs 42% in 2020 (fn 6), and 46.7% vs 46.6% in 2005/06 (Fig 29). Fig 38 is cited for a poverty number it does not show. Fig 5's source, World Bank 2023a, is the Kenya CEM; its decomposition data are unpublished.
- **Many text claims carry no source** (debt of 71.3% of GDP, 800,000 entrants a year, participation up 10 pp). A full engine also needs a claim-to-source checker.
- **Gaps compared with a typical jobs diagnostic.** The jobs side rests on ILO modelled estimates. There is:
  - no microdata labor-force analysis (KIHBS/KCHS);
  - nothing on employment status or informality beyond Fig 30's counts;
  - no gender, youth/NEET or age profiles;
  - no county breakdowns, despite the emphasis on devolution;
  - no earnings by education (Mincer premiums are text only);
  - no firm-size, unemployment or participation charts.
